## Executive Summary (150 words max)

This memo scopes a comprehensive financial ratio analysis of Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) for fiscal year 2024 (ended June 30, 2024). Microsoft is a global technology leader operating across enterprise software, cloud infrastructure (Azure), productivity platforms (Microsoft 365), gaming, and hardware. The objective is to evaluate Microsoft's financial health across six ratio categories: Performance, Profitability, Efficiency, Leverage, Liquidity, and DuPont decomposition. ## Company Background & Objectives

Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT) is a multinational technology conglomerate headquartered in Redmond, Washington, operating within the enterprise software and cloud services industry. The fiscal year under review is FY2024, ended June 30, 2024. Microsoft reports across three segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing.

A comprehensive ratio analysis is valuable for two principal reasons. First, Microsoft's revenue mix is rapidly shifting toward higher-margin, recurring cloud and SaaS streams — Azure grew 29% year-over-year in FY2024 — requiring updated efficiency and profitability benchmarks to accurately reflect underlying business economics. Second, the mid-year consolidation of Activision Blizzard has materially altered leverage and liquidity ratios, necessitating a fresh assessment before any equity model update. This analysis will equip the finance team with an empirical foundation to reassess valuation assumptions, stress-test capital structure scenarios, and contextualize Microsoft's performance against sector peers including Alphabet and Oracle.

## Approach

All financial inputs will be sourced from Microsoft's audited FY2024 Form 10-K (SEC EDGAR) and FY2022–2023 filings for trend analysis, supplemented by quarterly earnings press releases where additional granularity is required. Ratios will be organized into six categories:

- **Performance:** Revenue growth rate, EPS growth, and segment-level top-line trends to assess business momentum.
- **Profitability:** Gross margin, operating margin, net margin, and return on equity (ROE) to measure earnings quality.
- **Efficiency:** Asset turnover, receivables turnover, and days sales outstanding (DSO) to evaluate asset utilization.
- **Leverage:** Debt-to-equity, interest coverage ratio, and net debt to quantify capital structure risk post-acquisition.
- **Liquidity:** Current ratio, quick ratio, and cash ratio to assess near-term solvency and operational flexibility.
- **DuPont Decomposition:** Three-factor breakdown of ROE into net profit margin, asset turnover, and equity multiplier to isolate the primary drivers of shareholder value creation.

Results will be presented in a standardized ratio table per category, with a three-year trend column (FY2022–2024) and a peer benchmark column. A plain-language interpretation section will accompany each category to support non-technical readers.

## Limitations & Next Steps

Several constraints apply. Microsoft does not fully disclose segment-level balance sheet data, limiting the precision of efficiency ratios at the business unit level. The mid-year Activision consolidation introduces year-over-year comparability challenges; affected metrics will be adjusted on a pro-forma basis where sufficient disclosure exists. All ratios reflect historical reported figures and carry no forward-looking implication.
